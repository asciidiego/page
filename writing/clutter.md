---
TITLE: Clutter
AUTHOR: Diego Rodriguez
---

A cluttered mind is fertile soil for wrong ideas.

Our world is complex but our minds can help us navigate it.  But
unlike other vehicles or tools of modern age, we can't easily replace
it; we can only try to repair it.

But, how does one take care of one's mind?  In the same way we take
care of our body.

Assuming our mind is in reasonable shape, we can improve our minds
in the same way we improve reasonably fit bodies: training [^1].
Though, as with most worthy things in life, the steps are simple
to list but hard to follow:

- train it;

- nourish it;

- and let it rest and grow.


# Footnotes

[^1]: You may be wondering what can we do with extremely unhealthy
minds.  Well, I think we can stretch the analogy further here too
and ask the following question: what can one do with an extremely
unhealthy body?  The answer to such hairy question is "it depends".
